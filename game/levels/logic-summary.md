Curry–Howard turned logic into programming. A proposition is a type, a proof is a value, and every tautology here was a function you wrote: implication is `→`, conjunction is `prod`, truth is `Unit`. Negation and disjunction wait on user-defined inductive types.

Next: identity types, where the propositions are equalities and their proofs start to behave like paths.
