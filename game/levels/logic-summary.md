Curry–Howard turned logic into programming. A proposition is a type, a proof is a value, and every tautology here was a function you wrote: implication is `→`, conjunction is `prod`, disjunction is `coprod`, truth is `Unit`, falsity is `Void`, and the quantifiers are `Π` and `Σ`.

Next: identity types, where the propositions are equalities and their proofs start to behave like paths.
