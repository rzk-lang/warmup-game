One dimension up from a morphism is a **triangle**. Where a morphism is a map out of the interval `Δ¹`, a triangle is a map out of the 2-simplex `Δ²`, the shape `\ (t , s) → s ≤ t` carved out of the square.

A triangle `hom2 A x y z f g h` witnesses that the morphism `h : x → z` is a composite of `f : x → y` and `g : y → z`, by filling the triangle whose three edges are `f`, `g`, and `h`.

*By the end of this section you will be able to:* fill the constant triangle at a point.
