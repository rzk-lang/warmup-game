You built the machinery of sameness: homotopy (functions equal pointwise), equivalence (a function with a two-sided inverse), and `Equiv` (types with an equivalence between them). The identity is an equivalence, so every type is equivalent to itself. Univalence then identifies equality of types with equivalence, the defining axiom of univalent foundations.
