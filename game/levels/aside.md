A short aside, sitting mid-section as a `role: note` page. Prose may appear anywhere in a section, not only at its ends.

The next puzzle is **gated**. A gated puzzle grants a fixed set of lemmas in its `inventory`, and additionally bans built-in eliminators through a `forbidden` list; it refuses a solution that reaches for anything else, even when that something type-checks. Use it when a puzzle is about building a thing by hand rather than calling a shortcut.
