A striking payoff of Σ-types: the **type-theoretic axiom of choice** is a theorem here, provable with nothing but pairing and projection. Read as logic, it says that if for every `x` there exists a `y` with `R x y`, then there is a single function picking such a `y` for each `x`.

That the "axiom" of choice is provable is a feature of the constructive reading of "exists": a proof that something exists already carries the chosen witness.

*By the end of this section you will be able to:* prove the type-theoretic axiom of choice.
