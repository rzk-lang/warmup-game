*By the end of this section you will have seen:* a puzzle that carries **hints**, a **pre-test** that offers **remedies** when you are stuck, and a follow-on puzzle that stays locked until the pre-test is satisfied.

None of it is hard. Each level exists to show one feature in isolation, so that the file behind it is a clean example to copy.
