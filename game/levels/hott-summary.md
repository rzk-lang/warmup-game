You have met the bottom of the homotopy hierarchy: contractible types, propositions, and sets, ordered by strength. You showed `Unit` is contractible and a proposition, that contractible types are propositions, that an inhabited proposition is contractible, and that the empty type is a set.

The next step, that every proposition is a set, is true and provable from just these path operations, though its proof is longer. Above sets the hierarchy continues, one *h-level* at a time.
