You have reached the edge of the warm-up. Rzk's directed types, with morphisms and triangles built from the interval and its shapes, are the start of *synthetic ∞-category theory*: composition, Segal types, isomorphisms, and ultimately the Yoneda lemma.

That journey is the subject of the **∞-Yoneda game**, which picks up exactly here. You now have every prerequisite: functions, pairs, logic, identity types, equivalences, and a first taste of the directed world.

Thanks for playing.
