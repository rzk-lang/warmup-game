Dependent pairs — **Σ-types** — are the last of the basic type formers, and they have a striking payoff. First you build Σ-types and their projections and eliminators. Then you prove the *type-theoretic axiom of choice*, a one-line theorem that falls straight out of them.

*By the end of this section you will be able to:* build and take apart a dependent pair, state its recursion and induction principles, and prove the type-theoretic axiom of choice.
